# Vesna Launcher Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Vesna Launcher (`dev.vladon.vesna.launcher`).

## What Vesna accesses and stores

Vesna uses Android’s list of launchable apps, including apps in a work profile when Android makes them available, to display, search, and open apps. To save your home-screen layout, Vesna stores information such as pinned app and profile identifiers, shortcut positions, folder names, and preferences in Android’s private app storage on your device.

Vesna does not require an account, show ads, or use analytics or tracking. Vesna does not transmit this launcher data to the developer or share it with advertisers.

## Android backup and data deletion

Android system backup is enabled for Vesna. Depending on your Android version, device, and settings, Android’s backup service may copy eligible launcher data to your configured backup provider or transfer it to another device. Vesna does not operate that service or receive those copies.

Your local launcher data remains in Android’s private app storage until it is removed in the app, you clear Vesna’s storage in Android Settings, or you uninstall Vesna. Clearing local data does not necessarily delete existing backup copies. Use your device or backup provider’s settings to manage those copies.
